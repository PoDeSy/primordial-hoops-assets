Hoops, before bones

Primordial Hoops is a tabletop basketball game set in the warm, glowing soup where life began. Your players are single-celled microbes, the ball is a pulsing energy orb, and the hoop is a ring of light drifting in the current.

The rules, player count and components are still being shaped. Subscribers hear first, and may get a shot at playtesting.
